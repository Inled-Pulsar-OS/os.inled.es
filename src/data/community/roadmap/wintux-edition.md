A Windows-inspired edition of Pulsar OS for people coming from Windows.

**What we are thinking about:**
- Centered taskbar, start menu and snap layouts.

**Open decision — we need your input:** should Wintux be built on **KDE
Plasma** or **GNOME**? We are leaning towards KDE Plasma for a closer
Windows feel, but we want the community to weigh in on Discord and Matrix.
