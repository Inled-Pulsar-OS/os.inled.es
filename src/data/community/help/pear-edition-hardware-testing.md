Pear Edition is in alpha and we need real hardware reports before
recommending it on a daily driver.

**What we need:**
- Boot the latest ISO on your machine.
- Report the hardware that works and what doesn't.
- Share dmesg and `lspci` output when something fails.
- Keep a short list of reproducible issues.

Even one test per machine helps. Reports are tracked in the project board
and every tester is credited.
